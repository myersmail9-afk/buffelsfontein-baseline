Buffelsfontein: what's already on record

Every public sighting and specimen recorded inside Buffelsfontein Game and Nature Reserve, from iNaturalist, eBird, Observation.org, Birda, DNA barcode collections and herbaria, plus the Southern African Bird Atlas for the surrounding area. All of it on one map and one baseline you can check. Each number opens the exact records behind it, and each record links back to where it came from.

Where records have been made

Turn sources on and off below. Squares (about 250 m) hold records with a stated GPS accuracy of 250 m or better. Circles hold records whose location is only approximate: a birding hotspot, a checklist start point, or accuracy not stated. Tap any shape for a summary and its records. Bird-atlas squares can be switched on with the layer button at top right.

Where this data comes from

Every record on this page comes from one of the public sources below. Each one is named, with its publisher, licence and a link back. Nothing here is estimated or made up. Records from iNaturalist were pulled from iNaturalist directly. All other sighting and specimen records come through GBIF, the Global Biodiversity Information Facility, an intergovernmental open-data network that South Africa takes part in through SANBI. Only records whose coordinates fall inside the reserve's boundary are included.

Recording effort (iNaturalist)

A baseline depends on who is looking and how often. This section uses iNaturalist only, the one source here where each record has its own observer and identification status. Observers are shown as letters, ranked by number of records.

ID backlog

"Needs ID" iNaturalist records have a photo but no community-confirmed name yet. Confirming them is the cheapest way to strengthen the baseline.

Protected and near-threatened species

Species with a threatened or near-threatened status (on iNaturalist, or on the IUCN Red List category GBIF attaches to a record). In a wildlife-crime setting, this page never shows where they were seen. They're left off the map, and no coordinates are kept for them.
